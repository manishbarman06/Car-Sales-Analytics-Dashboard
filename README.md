**Author:** Executive Analytics Team  
**Tooling:** Power BI Desktop | DAX | Data Modeling  
**Dataset Scope:** 10,000 Vehicle Transactions | Multi-Territory Operations  

---

## 1. Business Context & Strategic Objectives
The automotive retail landscape requires real-time insight into sales velocity, territory expansion, inventory turnover, and sales force productivity. The primary objective of this project was to design, construct, and deploy an executive-grade **Car Sales Analytics Dashboard** in Power BI. 

The report enables C-suite leadership (CEO, CFO, VP of Sales) to answer three core operational questions:
1. **Financial Health:** Are top-line revenues supported by healthy gross profit margins and disciplined discounting?
2. **Geographic Dynamics:** Which regions lead in YoY expansion, and where are performance bottlenecks occurring?
3. **Sales Efficiency:** How effectively are sales representatives converting volume into profit without eroding margin?

## 3. Key Findings & Diagnostic Insights

### A. Regional YoY Velocity & Market Share
* **Growth Leaders:** The **North (41% YoY Growth)** and **Central (39% YoY Growth)** regions represent the fastest-growing market corridors, generating $10.66M and $10.31M in revenue respectively.
* **Margin Uniformity:** Gross profit margins remain exceptionally tight across all five sales territories (**18.38% to 18.59%**), indicating unified pricing structures nationwide.
* **Transmission Preference:** Automatic transmissions account for **>70% of preference share across all territories**, while CVT (~19%) and Manual (~10%) fulfill secondary niche demand.

### B. Product & Powertrain Dynamics
* **Revenue Anchor:** High-end trim levels in SUVs and Sedans maintain strong pricing power, holding the overall business ASP steady at $52.6K.
* **Discount Sensitivity:** Volume-focused trim levels require minor promotional incentives, but overall discounting remains well under the 8% risk threshold at 6.40%.

### C. Sales Force Productivity & Pricing Realization
* **Top Rep Volume:** Top sales representatives (e.g., Reese Witherspoon, Riley Davis) generate between **$3.28M and $3.30M+** individually with volume outputs ranging from 620 to 640+ units.
* **Margin Preservation:** Top performers maintain high gross profit contributions (~$60M–$62M total) without over-relying on aggressive price discounting.

## 4. Strategic Recommendations & Action Plan

1. **Replicate North/Central Expansion Playbooks:** Analyze marketing mix and promotional campaigns in the North (41% YoY) and Central (39% YoY) regions to apply similar playbooks in the East territory (33% YoY).
2. **Standardize Rep Upselling Frameworks:** Pair mid-tier sales reps with top performers to share upselling strategies for high-margin trim packages, driving team-wide ASP closer to $55K.
3. **Inventory Alignment with Powertrain Demand:** Maintain an 80/20 inventory ratio favoring Automatic/CVT powertrains in regional hubs to reduce floor-plan days to sell.
